Building AI scientists that scale research attention.

Research Lead at Amazon · Self-improving AI · ex–Carnegie Mellon, Microsoft Research

I am building A-EVOLVE around one thesis: compute converts into capability through research attention—the input that does not scale.

We build AI scientists that make it scale: frontier agents and models playing the researcher role end to end, improving both agent harnesses and model weights. The longer arc is bespoke agents and bespoke model training without staffing a research team per model—and, as the loop compounds, faster frontier-model development itself.
