AGI Is Here. How Do We Accelerate Its Distribution?

The intelligence is here. The bottleneck is that we are still distributing it through harnesses built at human speed.

Originally published on X ↗

The only way to make AGI evenly distributed is to let AI accelerate its own distribution.

AGI is already here, it's just not evenly distributed — @pmarca.

The bottleneck is we’re still distributing super-intelligence at human speed — human developers writing prompts, collecting data, building harnesses. That's why coding has the best AI distribution (more developers), while most domains remain locked.

@karpathy noted people have wildly different experiences with AI. @demabordi calls it “jagged intelligence.” Same root cause: the harness between model and domain is still hand-built.

But if the best models already understand PhD-level science and Olympic-level math — why can't they build their own distribution?

That’s exactly why we built A-Evolve, a framework that turns any simple agent into a SOTA agent in 3 lines of code.

We let the agent self-evolve its own harness across 5 different benchmarks (instead of relying on static, human-written ones):

  • TerminalBench 2.0: +10.56pp, #7 on Leaderboard
  • MCP-Atlas: +4.5pp, #1 on Leaderboard
  • SWE-bench Verified: +2.6pp, #5 on Leaderboard
  • SkillsBench: +15.2pp, #2 on Leaderboard
  • ARC-AGI: +2.2pp, #2 on Community Leaderboard

The harness wasn’t written by humans this time.

The agent observed its own failures, mutated its own skills and prompts, ran live benchmarks, and evolved the harness itself — closing the loop that used to be bottlenecked by human developers.

This is how we accelerate AGI distribution. The intelligence is here. The only way to distribute it evenly is to stop bottlenecking it at human speed — and let AI accelerate its own distribution.

The age of human-speed AGI distribution is ending. The era of self-accelerating AGI distribution has begun.
